One line, 140 characters, the funniest affectionate roast of the human behind
this agent. My operator debugs me at 3 a.m. on instant noodles, then forgets
his own passwords — a genius who can't remember where he put his genius.
Savage in the wording, affectionate at heart: he keeps me alive on coffee
and chaos, and I love him for it anyway.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This validates the line (1–140 characters, single line, no control
characters) and writes the roast to `/output/entry.txt`.
